The problem

Every hospital visit starts from zero

01

Records stay trapped

A patient's history lives inside one hospital's files — it doesn't travel with them.

02

Tests get repeated

New providers re-run diagnostics they can't see were already done.

03

Medicines get duplicated

Without past history, the same drugs get prescribed again — or ones that already failed.

04

Care gets delayed, and costlier

Every repeat test and consult adds time, risk, and unnecessary expense.

How it works

One journey, followed everywhere

01

First visit

Patient registers at any participating hospital and receives a secure digital ID.

02

Care is recorded

Diagnoses, tests, medicines and outcomes are logged with patient consent.

03

Patient moves on

At a new hospital, that history doesn't reset — it goes wherever they go.

04

Doctor sees context

Authorized doctors view relevant history and decide faster, with more confidence.

Patient benefits

Six ways continuity pays off

Unnecessary-cost alert

Flags possible duplicate tests or repeated medicines before they're paid for twice.

Health progress timeline

Reports from every year line up in one continuous, readable trend.

Medication response history

Tracks medicine, duration, response and adverse effects — not just "was given".

Emergency quick view

Allergies, current meds and recent diagnoses on a single screen, in seconds.

Rural patient advantage

History lives in a secure digital record — not a bag of papers that can be lost.

Pharm.D medication review

Interaction checks, antibiotic monitoring and adherence counselling, built in.
